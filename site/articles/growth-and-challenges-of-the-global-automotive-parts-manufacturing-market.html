Growth and Challenges of the Global Automotive Parts Manufacturing Market
advertisement

The global automotive parts manufacturing market is experiencing continuous growth, with an expected market size of $71.02 billion in 2025, projected to reach $110.47 billion by 2034, at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 5.03%. As an upstream industry of the automobile manufacturing sector, the automotive parts industry plays a crucial role in supporting and influencing the development of the automotive sector.

Driving Factors Behind Growth

Several key factors are driving this growth. The continuous rise in the global vehicle population serves as a fundamental basis. With economic development, more consumers can afford automobiles. As of 2019, the global vehicle population was approximately 1.4 billion, increasing to around 1.55 billion in 2024. This massive vehicle base provides a stable demand for automotive parts.

According to the 2024 *Automotive News* ranking of the top 100 global automotive parts suppliers, the top five suppliers come from Japan, the United States, Germany, China, and South Korea. This ranking reflects the rapid transformation of the automotive supply chain in the era of electrification, with both traditional suppliers and emerging players competing for a fast-growing electric vehicle (EV) market, leading to intensified industry competition.

As the global automotive industry undergoes innovation-driven transformation, the automotive parts sector, as a key link in the industry chain, is demonstrating unprecedented growth potential and market vitality. In this industry boom, how can companies grasp market trends and seize opportunities?

The automotive parts industry covers numerous segments with a wide variety of products, resulting in relatively low overall industry concentration. However, with product upgrades, structural optimization, and the integration of the industrial chain, market concentration is expected to gradually increase. Additionally, the performance and quality of automotive parts directly affect the safety, reliability, and functionality of vehicles. Therefore, automobile manufacturers have strict certification processes for suppliers. Due to the long supplier certification cycles of vehicle manufacturers and the significant initial investment required, once an automotive parts company becomes a certified supplier, it typically establishes a stable cooperative relationship with the manufacturer. This creates high market entry barriers, especially for new entrants, as the certification process itself is a major challenge.

Market Growth Drivers

The growth of the automotive parts manufacturing market is primarily driven by the following factors:

- Demand for Fuel Efficiency and Sustainability: Governments worldwide are implementing policies and regulations to reduce carbon emissions, prompting automakers to invest in more environmentally friendly automotive technologies, such as improved engine components, electrical systems, and lightweight materials to enhance fuel economy and reduce environmental pollution.

- Expansion of the Electric Vehicle Market: The increasing adoption of electric vehicles is driving demand for key components such as batteries, electric drive systems, and green cooling systems.

- Rising Consumer Environmental Awareness: Consumers are increasingly demanding greener, energy-efficient, and environmentally friendly vehicles, pushing the parts industry toward more sustainable product development.

Challenges Facing the Industry

Despite the promising market outlook, the automotive parts manufacturing industry faces multiple challenges. Firstly, to meet the high standards of fuel-efficient and electric vehicles, manufacturers must invest heavily in research and development and use high-quality materials, which significantly raises production costs. Secondly, the industry is highly dependent on raw materials such as steel, aluminum, and rare earth metals. Global trade tensions and disruptions caused by the COVID-19 pandemic have led to supply chain bottlenecks, resulting in production delays and increased costs. Finally, labor shortages in certain regions, coupled with rising green manufacturing requirements, make it more challenging for companies to maintain profitability while delivering high-quality, cost-effective products.

The global automotive parts manufacturing market is in a phase of rapid growth, driven by improvements in fuel efficiency, the widespread adoption of electric vehicles, and environmental policies. However, companies must navigate rising manufacturing costs, supply chain instability, and high market entry barriers. In the future, as the industry structure optimizes and technology advances, market concentration is expected to increase, leading to further shifts in the competitive landscape.
