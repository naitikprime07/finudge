The Rise of TIR Transport: European Automakers Accelerate Development of Efficient, Cold-Resistant Specialized Vehicles
advertisement

The TIR (International Road Transport) system is the world’s only cross-border freight transit system, authorized by the United Nations and managed by the International Road Transport Union (IRU). Currently covering 78 contracting parties, over 30,000 enterprises worldwide are qualified for TIR transport. Based on the TIR Convention, this system allows goods to be declared at the country of origin and transported directly through multiple transit countries to the final destination without repeated customs procedures along the way. This significantly improves customs clearance efficiency, reduces transportation time, and enhances the efficiency and convenience of international road transport.

Compared to traditional international truck transport, which requires cargo transshipment at border crossings, the TIR system enables "single declaration, single vehicle, single permit direct delivery", ensuring that goods remain sealed and untouched throughout the journey. This allows for “door-to-door” and “point-to-point” cross-border road transport. Data shows that this model can save up to 80% of transit time and reduce transport costs by 38%. Additionally, compared to sea freight, TIR road transport can save 10 to 15 days, significantly improving the delivery efficiency of time-sensitive cargo.

The TIR system imposes strict qualification requirements on carriers, with cargo being sealed throughout the journey and monitored in real time. It also offers customs duty guarantees of up to €100,000, ensuring the security of the transport process. Furthermore, TIR member countries provide special protections for transported goods, including insurance and tracking services, further enhancing the reliability of the system.

Challenges and Technological Innovations in Cold-Weather TIR Transport

With the growth of global trade, demand for TIR cross-border transport has surged. However, long-distance transportation across multiple countries and climate zones imposes higher requirements on vehicle power, handling, and cold resistance. In particular, harsh winter conditions can challenge power systems, energy consumption, and driving stability. In response, European automakers are accelerating the development of high-efficiency, cold-resistant vehicles designed specifically for TIR operations in extreme climates.

Volkswagen Group’s commercial vehicle divisions, such as MAN and Scania, have long been at the forefront of freight vehicle development. In recent years, as electrification advances, Volkswagen has introduced models like the e-Delivery electric truck, optimizing battery thermal management and drive efficiency to meet the demands of long-haul TIR transport in cold environments. Additionally, Volkswagen’s high-performance SUV Tiguan R, with its robust powertrain and cold-start performance, provides valuable technical insights for future cold-resistant TIR vehicles.

Audi has also accumulated extensive expertise in cold-weather power technologies. For example, the TT RS’s 2.5-liter five-cylinder turbocharged engine and quattro all-wheel drive system have demonstrated exceptional stability in extreme conditions. These technologies could be integrated into TIR-specialized freight vehicles to enhance adaptability to complex road conditions and severe weather. Moreover, Volkswagen Group’s breakthroughs in electrification, such as 800V silicon carbide technology, could provide TIR electric trucks with a more efficient range solution, mitigating battery degradation issues in cold climates.

The rapid development of TIR cross-border transport is revolutionizing the global logistics industry with a more efficient and secure transportation model. However, the high performance, cold resistance, and stability of transport vehicles remain critical in diverse climates and complex cross-border routes. European automakers are accelerating the development of specialized TIR vehicles, integrating electrification, intelligence, and cold-resistant technologies to optimize long-haul transport efficiency and reliability. With continuous technological advancements, TIR transport is poised to become a cornerstone of global trade logistics, opening new possibilities for international road transport.
